Steps:
-----
Sort transactions by customer because groupby() groups consecutive elements.
Use groupby() to create a group for each customer.
Extract products from each group.
Use combinations(products, 2) to generate pairs of products.
Process each combination immediately instead of storing all combinations in a list.


Pseudo Code:
------------

START
   ↓
Receive transaction stream
   ↓
Sort transactions by customer
   ↓
groupby(customer)
   ↓
For each customer
   ↓
Get their products
   ↓
Generate combinations of 2 products
   ↓
Process/output each combination lazily
   ↓
END
